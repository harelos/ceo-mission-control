## The one-sentence decision

**Stop using agents mainly as extensions of the founder; build three outcome-owning departments that can choose the next permitted action from a persistent backlog, verify their own work, and escalate only true CEO decisions.**

## What to do next

Do not hire ten more agents.

Today/next implementation sequence:

- verify actual Foundry runtime/auth/quota telemetry;
- create persistent department backlog/state;
- freeze the three department charters and QA rules;
- pilot Growth first;
- make the Meta Manager maintain its own testing backlog and creative pipeline;
- keep live Meta writes CEO-approved until the bounded testing policy is explicitly activated;
- run 72 hours and measure accepted output, quota use and CEO interruptions;
- expand authority only when the data proves the workflow is reliable.

## CEO rule

Whenever the CEO is about to do operational work, ask:

> **Is this actually a CEO decision?**

If not:

> **Who owns this, and what policy or system is missing that made it reach me?**
