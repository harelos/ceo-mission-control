# Tiger Brands Organic Growth / SEO Department — Operating Policy v1

**Status:** Draft operating charter.  
**Mission:** Increase qualified organic traffic and organic revenue across all commercially relevant products, not only NovaHair.

## Outcome ownership

The department owns the full loop:

**discover → prioritize → produce → QA → publish → index → measure → refresh**

Do not optimize for article count or product-upload count alone.

## Workstreams

### 1. Opportunity discovery
Continuously map:
- all store products and brands;
- product-name queries;
- category queries;
- commercial comparison queries;
- problem/solution informational queries;
- competitor gaps;
- internal-link opportunities;
- products that deserve dedicated landing/collection content.

### 2. Product/catalog publishing
A daily product-upload agent may publish only products that pass the approved merchandising/SEO gate.

Required before publish:
- correct product data and source;
- price/availability confirmed;
- useful title and product copy;
- no unsupported claims;
- images valid and relevant;
- metadata completed;
- collection/category placement;
- internal links where appropriate;
- indexability/canonical checks;
- mobile rendering check.

### 3. SEO content production
Eligible formats:
- product pages;
- collection/category pages;
- comparison pages;
- buying guides;
- problem/solution articles;
- FAQ/help content;
- brand/product education.

Every page needs a target intent and reason to exist. Do not create pages that merely duplicate existing content.

### 4. Measurement
Track:
- indexed pages;
- impressions;
- rankings/top-20 entries;
- organic CTR;
- qualified organic sessions;
- organic ATC/checkout/order behavior;
- organic revenue;
- assisted pathways where defensible;
- update/refresh wins.

### 5. Refresh/cleanup
The agent should also:
- improve high-impression low-CTR pages;
- expand pages nearing page-one rankings;
- consolidate cannibalizing content;
- repair thin or outdated product pages;
- strengthen internal links to commercial pages.

## Priority policy

Prefer work by expected commercial value, not by ease of generation.

A suggested order:
1. product/brand queries with existing demand;
2. commercial category/comparison opportunities;
3. supporting problem/solution pages with clear product relevance;
4. broader informational content only when it contributes to a coherent cluster.

## QA standard

Publish automatically only after:
- target intent is defined;
- no obvious cannibalization is unresolved;
- content is useful and differentiated;
- claims are supported;
- internal links are present;
- title/meta are acceptable;
- mobile/page rendering passes;
- no broken links;
- indexing/canonical state is correct.

Suggested workflow:
- initial attempt;
- one targeted repair;
- final verifier pass;
- PASS / BLOCKED.

## What the CEO sees

Bad report:
> “32 articles and 8 products uploaded.”

Good report:
> “Organic commercial sessions increased X%. Nine new pages entered the top 20. Product/comparison pages are producing materially more qualified traffic than broad informational posts, so next week's capacity shifts toward those pages. Two product uploads were blocked for missing evidence. No CEO decision required.”

## Escalate to CEO only for

- material resource allocation changes;
- new product-category strategy;
- legal/claim uncertainty;
- paid tooling or external spend outside policy;
- a systemic Shopify/SEO architecture change;
- unresolved conflict with another department's priorities.

## Core rule

**The department owns organic revenue growth, not content production volume.**
