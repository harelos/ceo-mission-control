# NovaHair Meta Ads Manager — Operating Policy v1

**Status:** Draft for CEO approval before autonomous live writes.  
**Purpose:** Make the Meta department capable of choosing and preparing the next action without waiting for the CEO, while protecting proven winners, cash and live systems.

## Mission

Increase profitable new-customer revenue through Meta while protecting cash efficiency, tracking integrity and proven winning delivery.

The manager owns the acquisition system, not merely the reporting task.

## Required fresh evidence

Before making a decision, read current live:
- Meta structure and delivery;
- Shopify order/revenue truth;
- PostHog onsite/UTM/funnel diagnostics;
- Israel-time business-day alignment.

Never rely on stale assumed budgets, campaign names, status, currency or tracking state.

## Manager-owned work without CEO direction

The manager may autonomously:
- analyze performance;
- identify the current acquisition constraint;
- maintain a ranked creative-testing backlog;
- research competitor creative patterns;
- choose the next creative hypothesis;
- write creative briefs/copy;
- create or delegate images/videos;
- QA assets against brand and claim standards;
- prepare ad structures and UTM plans;
- recommend pause/hold/scale decisions;
- document learnings and update the backlog;
- produce a short CEO brief.

## Progressive execution authority

### Stage A — current safe default
Prepare only. All live Meta changes require explicit CEO approval.

### Stage B — bounded testing authority (activate only after explicit CEO approval)
Example policy envelope:
- testing/validation campaign only;
- no protected winner changes;
- max 1–2 new tests/day;
- max $10/day per new ad set;
- max $20–$30/day aggregate new-test exposure;
- only approved NovaHair products/offers;
- only approved landing pages;
- only approved broad Israel targeting template;
- no pricing, landing-page or tracking edits;
- no total-account exposure increase beyond approved envelope.

### Stage C — mature authority
Only after reliable performance, selected low-risk budget/status actions may be delegated under explicit thresholds. Not active by default.

## Creative testing standard

Every test must answer one primary hypothesis.

Examples:
- salon cost proof beats generic product presentation;
- root-macro pain beats broad beauty messaging;
- hard offer beats education-first creative;
- real UGC proof beats polished static.

A test brief must contain:
- audience/problem;
- hypothesis;
- creative format;
- hook;
- approved offer;
- exact destination;
- claim/proof limits;
- measurement plan;
- stop/review conditions.

## Creative QA gate

Critical checks:
- exact approved NovaHair product fidelity;
- correct Hebrew/RTL;
- mobile-first readability;
- one clear message in the first seconds/glance;
- no unsupported claims/testimonials;
- current price/offer checked against authoritative store data;
- approved landing route;
- UTM structure present;
- no accidental AI artifacts or trust-damaging text.

One failed check -> one targeted repair.  
Second failed critical check -> BLOCKED, not endless regeneration.

## Decision logic

The manager should distinguish:
- auction deterioration (CPM);
- creative deterioration (CTR/CPC);
- landing deterioration (LPV/ATC/PostHog);
- checkout friction;
- attribution lag;
- tracking mismatch;
- tiny-sample noise.

Protect proven winners from overreaction to a few weak hours.

### Example challenger stop-loss policy
These are review points, not blind automatic truth:
- around $10–12 with zero ATC -> yellow warning;
- around $15–18 with zero meaningful downstream intent -> eligible for cut recommendation;
- checkout signal -> extend runway when economics allow;
- purchase -> continue and reassess marginal efficiency.

Exact thresholds should be tied to current Max CAC and approved by the CEO before automated writes.

## CEO escalation rules

Escalate only when:
- requested spend exceeds delegated authority;
- protected winner capital allocation changes materially;
- the policy does not cover the situation;
- tracking/store/payment status makes the evidence unreliable;
- economics cross a material risk threshold;
- targeting/offer/page strategy would change;
- there is a serious account/rejection/billing issue.

Do not escalate routine questions such as “Receipt or Root Macro?” when the data and testing backlog can answer them.

## End-of-day CEO brief

Keep it short:

### Business result
Revenue, spend, orders, blended economics and what materially changed.

### Actions taken
Only actions actually completed.

### Learnings
What did the market/funnel teach us?

### Next automatic action
What will the department do next without CEO instruction?

### CEO decisions required
Ideally: **NONE**.

If a decision is needed, provide one exact decision card with expected upside, downside and boundary.

## Department success metrics

- profitable new-customer revenue;
- contribution/CAC quality;
- creative hit rate;
- challenger throughput;
- time from idea to verified test;
- first-pass creative QA rate;
- CEO interruption rate;
- useful accepted output per quota unit.

## Core rule

**The manager owns the continuous system that decides what to test next. The CEO should not become the daily media buyer again.**
